# Journey and progression — 1.4.0

Emberhall is a guild-building game with automatic combat. The player's decisions are who to recruit, how to equip
and develop them, which doctrine suits an encounter, and when the party is ready for a boss. Auto team recommends
a formation from available heroes using the actual encounter simulator; it does not start or chain battles.

## The loop

1. Read the current chapter objective and choose a trail on the Campaign board.
2. Compare the enemy formation, party health, forecast and doctrine. Auto team can help select the formation.
3. Start one automatic encounter. Attacks and skills resolve through every wave; pause, speed and skip control playback.
4. Use loot, training, healing, promotion and equipment to improve the guild. Retry trails for stars when needed.
5. Earn eight stars across the region's four trails, defeat its boss, and meet the next guild-rank requirement.
6. Open the next region and continue the five-lens story. The Spire offers an escalating optional challenge.

Stars reward performance, not simply entering a stage. The boss requires 8 of the 12 available trail stars, so the
player can progress without perfecting every trail. Previously cleared bosses keep their clear after an update.
Region unlocks require both their existing rank and the preceding region's boss; rank alone no longer skips the story.

## Stamina

| Rule | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Maximum reserve | 30 |
| Recovery, including offline | 1 every 5 minutes; a full empty reserve takes 150 minutes |
| First clear of each of the first three Sunmeadow trails | Free, including retries until first victory |
| Other trails, including replays | 2 |
| Bosses and Spire floors | 3 |
| Defeat or an interrupted paid encounter | At most 1; the remainder is returned |
| Guild exams and contracts | Free |
| Camp ration | Free +10, once per UTC day, usable at 20 or less |

Opening a party screen, asking for Auto team or previewing odds never spends stamina. Payment happens once when
the encounter actually starts. A saved interrupted encounter returns the unused portion on load and cannot grant
battle rewards. Recovery stops at the cap; time spent full cannot be stockpiled. Old guild saves begin with a full
reserve. Stamina and camp receipts are local save data, consistent with the rest of this offline game.

The reserve limits repeated combat farming, while boss mastery and guild development provide the challenge.
Training, forging, healing, research, contracts, the Treasury, quests and exams remain available while it recovers.
There is no paid stamina refill or mandatory rewarded advertisement. Camp supplies require no advertisement.

## What Auto does

**Auto team** chooses ready heroes. **Start auto battle** starts one encounter and plays all of its waves.
The next trail requires a player choice. Speed and skip shorten presentation without changing the already simulated
outcome, loot or stamina cost. Increasing the playback speed therefore does not bypass progression requirements.

These are the initial pacing values. Regression checks cover payments, recovery, interrupted runs, unlock gates and
automatic completion; they do not establish a measured campaign completion time or replace longer balance playtests.
