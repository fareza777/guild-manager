Pixel Game Master · Emberhall

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Emberhall stores your guild name, heroes, inventory, progress, settings, remove-ads entitlement cache and video-bonus receipts on your device. The game has no sign-in, cloud save or developer-operated analytics backend. We do not send your guild name or save file to an advertising service. Reset Progress removes the active guild save; uninstalling the app removes its local app data, subject to your device's backup settings.

Advertising and consent

Android builds include Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Test builds may use Google's sample ad units, which show test advertisements and do not earn advertising revenue. An internet connection is required for ads; the game can otherwise be played offline.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics and device/account identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Data sent by that SDK is encrypted in transit. See Google's SDK data disclosure, Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising technologies.

The game asks UMP for the applicable consent status before requesting ads. Where Google requires a consent form, it is presented before ad requests. Settings → Ads & privacy provides Manage privacy choices when UMP requires that entry point. Device advertising-ID controls are available in Android settings.

Video rewards are optional. Base battle rewards do not require an ad. A completed video earns the bonus described on its button; closing a video early or failing to load one does not consume game currency.

In-app purchase

The optional Remove ads non-consumable purchase is offered through Google Play Billing for USD 4.99 (the final localized price is set by Google Play). A successful purchase removes banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements on the Google Play account. Google Play processes payment, order, tax and refund information under its own privacy policy. The game receives only the purchase result needed to grant the entitlement; it does not store payment details. Restore purchase uses Google Play's account-level purchase history.

External links and support

Share, rating and privacy links open services outside the game, which have their own privacy practices. The game does not read your contacts or ask for camera, microphone or precise-location access.

For a privacy question, contact the maintainer through the project issue tracker. Do not post private information in a public issue; request a private contact channel first. We may update this policy when the game's data practices change.
