# Emberhall: Guild Legends — Privacy policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026. Developer: Pixel Game Master.

Emberhall stores your guild name, heroes, inventory, progress, settings, remove-ads entitlement cache and
video-bonus receipts on your device. The game has no sign-in, cloud save or developer-operated analytics backend.
We do not send your guild name or save file to an advertising service. Reset Progress removes the active guild
save; uninstalling the app removes its local app data, subject to your device's backup settings.

## Advertising and consent

Android builds from version 1.3.0 include Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). This release uses
Google sample ad units, which show test advertisements and do not earn advertising revenue. Test mode still uses
Google's network services. An internet connection is required for ads; the game can otherwise be played offline.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics and device/account
identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Data sent by that SDK is encrypted in transit.
See [Google's SDK data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure),
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[Google advertising technologies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) for details.

The game asks UMP for the applicable consent status before requesting ads. Where Google requires a consent form,
it is presented before ad requests. Settings → Ads & privacy provides **Manage privacy choices** when UMP requires
that entry point. Device advertising-ID controls are available in Android settings. Resetting guild progress does
not reset Google's consent choices.

Video rewards are optional. Base battle rewards do not require an ad. A completed video earns the bonus described
on its button; closing a video early or failing to load one does not consume game currency.

## In-app purchase

The optional **Remove ads** non-consumable purchase is offered through Google Play Billing for USD 4.99 (the
final localized price is set by Google Play). A successful purchase removes the game's banner, interstitial and
rewarded advertisements on the Google Play account. Google Play processes the payment, order, tax and refund
information under its own privacy policy. The game receives only the purchase result needed to grant the
entitlement; it does not store payment details. Restore purchase uses Google Play's account-level purchase history.

## External links and support

Share, rating and privacy links open services outside the game, which have their own privacy practices. The game
does not read your contacts or ask for camera, microphone or precise-location access.

For a privacy question, contact the maintainer through the
[project's issue tracker](https://github.com/fareza777/guild-manager/issues). Do not post private information in a
public issue; request a private contact channel first. We may update this policy when the game's data practices
change; the current policy is linked from Settings → Ads & privacy.
